Questions people ask

Who is Ishan Karunaratne?

Ishan Karunaratne is a systems and network architect and Chief Technology Officer. He has more than two decades of experience across software engineering, systems architecture, cloud infrastructure, and technology leadership, and is currently CTO at Rehab Media Group. He is a US Army veteran who served in Operation Iraqi Freedom, holds a Bachelor's degree from Charles Sturt University, and writes the developer publication TechEarl.

What does he work on?

Software architecture and distributed systems, Linux and DevOps infrastructure, application security, and AI engineering. He built and operates DNS Checker, a domain intelligence platform that analyzes more than 240 million domains a day, and has published more than 790 technical articles at TechEarl.

Which Ishan Karunaratne is this?

Fair question, because there are several of us and search engines merge us enthusiastically. I am not the frontend engineer at Moresand Technologies, and I am not the founder and managing director of Sinhalite Holdings, the Sri Lankan hospitality and consumer-goods group written up in the Daily Mirror and Life Online. He appears to be doing very well. If you need compostable lunch sheets, that is the other one. If you need someone to work out why the database falls over at 3am, you are on the right page. The reliable way to tell us apart is my ORCID iD, 0009-0002-3088-0769, which is unique to me.

Is he available for work?

He is open to hands-on development work, which is still the part he enjoys most, as well as advisory work, technical due diligence, architecture review, and fractional CTO engagements. The fastest way to reach him is email.
