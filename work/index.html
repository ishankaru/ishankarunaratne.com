Built and still running

DNS Checker

Designed, written, and operated solo  ·  dnschkr.com

A domain intelligence platform that analyzes more than 240 million domains a day. I own every layer: ingestion, the Elasticsearch index, the API, the front end, and the servers it runs on. Nobody else has ever had a commit in it, which is either a good sign or a warning, depending on your view of bus factor. Most people meet it through the free DNS Checker, which pulls every record type for a domain in one pass, then the DNS propagation checker to watch a change land on resolvers around the world, and the email authentication tools for SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. Most of my Linux, DNS, and networking articles come from keeping it up.

Lipwalk

Privacy-first comment system

Threaded comments, reactions, and star ratings you embed with one script tag, funded by subscriptions instead of by watching the people reading your site. AI moderation, a REST API, and TypeScript SDKs. The moderation pipeline is the same AI engineering work I write about.

Daycare Index

14,333 licensed providers, Texas first

Daycare Index is a childcare directory covering 14,333 licensed providers across Texas. Parents search by city or ZIP, then compare programs and ages, hours, locations, and licensing and inspection information side by side. Listings are ordered on the record alone: nobody can buy a better position, and there is no sponsored tier. Directories like this and the lawyers directory below are where the database and search work gets exercised hardest.

What's Taller?

Height comparison tool, 293 real and fictional subjects at true scale

The quickest answer to "what's taller?" I could build. Drop Godzilla next to the Eiffel Tower, the Statue of Liberty next to Burj Khalifa, or your own height next to a T. rex, and the scene renders every subject at true relative scale. Each measurement carries its source, fictional heights say which version they come from, and any comparison can be shared as a link or downloaded as an image. Free, no account, and nothing you enter leaves your browser.

American Lawyers Directory

51 states and DC, 8 practice areas

An independent attorney directory built on state bar records rather than paid placement. No firm owns it and nobody can buy a better position in the listings.

Lyrics LK

10,225+ Sinhala songs, 3,390 artists  ·  lyrics-lk.com

Lyrics LK is the largest Sinhala lyrics archive on the web: 10,225 songs by 3,390 artists as of 13 September 2026, every one in Sinhala script and in Singlish, indexed by song, artist, and lyricist. Sri Lankan music was badly served online, so I built the archive I wanted to exist: searchable, checked against the recordings, corrected in the open, and with no popups between you and the words. It now answers more than 650,000 Sinhala lyric searches a month.

The songs people are searching for most this month are Chamara Weerasinghe's Mathakayan Obe lyrics, H.R. Jothipala's Hanga Gal Lene lyrics, Nimesh Chamika's Mandaram Handawe lyrics, Bachi Susan's Bambara Nade lyrics, and Pradeep Rangana's Pem Sihine lyrics, which between them draw more than 110,000 searches a quarter.

Chords LK

1,700+ Sinhala songs with guitar chords  ·  chordslk.com

Chords LK is the largest collection of Sinhala guitar chords online: more than 1,700 songs with chords and tabs as of 13 September 2026, with a built-in transposer so any song plays in your key. The companion to the lyrics archive, for the people actually trying to play the songs rather than just read along.

The most-played pages right now are Jayasri's Sheela chords, Sanuka Wickramasinghe's Saragaye chords, Bathiya and Santhush's Mal Pan Podak chords, and Priya Sooriyasena's Atha Ran Viman chords.

Dr Pepper Addiction

19 varieties, caffeine data, and a joke

Half the fun of this one is the joke and the other half is real. It catalogues every Dr Pepper variety with the caffeine and nutrition numbers, and takes the caffeine-dependence part more seriously than the name suggests. It is also the test bed for the structured data and search work I later apply to the bigger sites.
